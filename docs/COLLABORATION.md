# Team Collaboration Plan

The team meets twice weekly, using both online and in-person sessions, and communicates through WeChat between meetings. Exact meeting times and individual role assignments remain to be agreed. All five members participate in product decisions, clarification answers, document review, and approval. Run one shared AI-DLC workflow.

| Responsibility | Mid-semester duties | Final-project continuation | Assigned member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow & GitHub | Maintain v1.0.1 setup, operate the shared workflow, collect team responses, check audit/state records, coordinate commits and submission. Never approve on behalf of absent teammates. | Coordinate Construction order, integration, and repository delivery. | Unassigned |
| Product & Requirements | Lead discussion of deciding what to eat, audience and MVP boundaries; prepare vision and README product sections; lead Requirements Analysis review. | Manage scope and tradeoffs, product explanation and presentation narrative. | Unassigned |
| UX & User Stories | Prepare a realistic five-day/three-meal scenario; review personas, stories, normal flows and acceptance criteria. Include dynamic meal changes and confirmation before deductions. | User interface, interaction flow, and demo path. | Unassigned |
| Technical & LLM | Lead technical input, free-model evaluation, recipe retrieval approach, tool design, data flow, and illustrative examples; review Application Design and unit dependencies with workflow lead. | Model integration, backend tools, and core logic. | Unassigned |
| Evaluation & Delivery | Prepare expected outcomes early; review shortages, duplicate confirmations, cancellation, failed search/model calls, story-to-unit mapping; check rubric completeness. | Integration tests, model evaluation, and demo verification. | Unassigned |

These are review and coordination responsibilities, not permission to generate Inception artifacts outside the shared workflow. All named members are listed in README.md. Individual assignments should reflect team agreement, not the order of the member list.
